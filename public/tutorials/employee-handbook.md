# Northstar demo employee handbook

This is fictional sample data for the OpenAgent RAG tutorial.

## Equipment allowance

Full-time employees can expense up to USD 480 for home-office equipment per calendar year. Manager approval is required before purchase. Submit receipts within 30 days of purchase.

## Leave requests

Request planned leave at least 5 working days in advance. Your manager approves requests in the team calendar.

## Support hours

The internal IT help desk is available Monday through Friday, 09:00–17:00 UTC. This handbook does not specify weekend or emergency support.
